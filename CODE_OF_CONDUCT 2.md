# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, factual, and careful with personal,
confidential, and platform data. Harassment, doxxing, impersonation, fabricated
claims, credential sharing, and attempts to automate external account actions
are not acceptable.

Report a concern privately through the security/contact route in `SECURITY.md`.
Maintainers may remove content, pause a contribution, or restrict participation
to protect people and the project.
