# Governance

SignalForge is maintained as a small, human-controlled research prototype.
Maintainers may review and merge code, but no automated agent may publish to a
social account, approve a release, change the data boundary, or treat heuristic
scores as platform facts.

Changes that add persistence, identity, analytics, model calls, OAuth, platform
integrations, or external account actions require an explicit issue/PR scope,
privacy and security review, deterministic tests, and human maintainer approval.
The MIT license does not grant permission to use platform APIs or account data.
